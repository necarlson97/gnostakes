## Open slots

**Now-blank faces** (their text moved elsewhere): Alembic Servitor, Perennial Pruner, The Tallyman, Lethean Elder, Driveling Chemist, Feverish Scribe.

## Design lens
Cashing out the key incinerates the card, so **the companion is what you give up**. The best companions make "cash out now or keep milking it?" a real question:
- **Engines** you'd hate to lose: income, a persistent edge.
- **Things that pay off the cash-out itself**: they fire on gaining a key or on incinerating.
- **Bait** that helps opponents a little, so you want to cash out early.

## Pool (mix and match; also fits the blank faces)
### Cups / W
1. Once per round, spend 1W to reroll one die.
2. Whenever you discard a die, gain 1W.
3. On your first roll each Swig, you only need to commit 3 dice.
4. On Scheme start: gain 1W per empty villager slot.
5. When you spend W, you may also spend it as F.
6. Whenever you gain a key: regain a tooth. *(Fires on its own cash-out.)*

### Wands / F
7. Whenever any player incinerates a card: gain 1F.
8. Whenever you lose a tooth: gain 2F.
9. Your F faces also count as A.
10. Once per round, you may swap two Town Square villagers.
11. When this card is incinerated, every opponent loses 1F. *(Bait inverted: rewards cashing out.)*
12. Whenever you score Four of a Color: gain 1Q.

### Any suit
13. Your teeth count as tokens when wagering, and you lose them if you win.
14. On Scheme start, if you have the fewest keys: gain 2 of any one token.
15. Whenever you score a Triplet: gain 2 of any one token.
16. Whenever an opponent gains a key: gain 1 of any token.
17. While active, your opponents' wagers count 1 less.
18. Each Scry, you may request one extra villager with 3 tokens.
19. On Scheme start, you may invert one other villager of yours for free.
20. You may keep 1 die uncommitted until scoring, then commit it.
21. Whenever you claim a villager by wager, gain 1 tooth.
22. Opponents must pay you 1 token to request the Town Square slot nearest you.
23. When you score nothing, you may incinerate a villager to score Three Straight.
24. Bait: whenever you gain W/F (match the suit), the player to your left gains 1 too.
